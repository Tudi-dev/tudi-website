The hand-drawn line work and the aqua-and-yellow palette carry the "Reconstructing emotions" concept across
every piece. The symbol works on its own, paired with the logotype, or reduced to a single ink color.

The identity shipped production-ready: artwork for exterior signage, stationery, promotional items and
social media templates, along with a usage manual for the symbol and palette.
