Ixchel is the Maya goddess of weaving. The logotype's "x" resolves as a cross-stitch, and that same stitch
becomes the pattern running through packaging, labels and posts.

The fine linework and the wine-and-earth-tone palette give a handmade product a place on the shelf without
losing the warmth of the craft. The identity shipped with ready-to-publish social media templates.
